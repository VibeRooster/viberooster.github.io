Some of the best things people hatch are explanations.

A pricing model mapped for one board meeting. A competitive landscape built to win one pitch. A causal diagram made to settle one argument. We call these explorables — interactive artifacts the reader navigates instead of reads. Expand a node, filter the set, drill from the overview into the one case you care about. The reader controls the projection, instead of receiving a snapshot someone else chose.

AI can now emit a working interactive artifact about as cheaply as a static picture. That's why hatch it, share it, let it expire is a first-class way to publish here — not an edge case.
